# Bun-Heist
Maven the Hedgehog (Disclaimer: not associated with Apache Maven) is eyeing the warm steamy hot cross buns at the bakery...but he doesn't have any money!! And worse, the baker is very protective of his hot cross buns.
Can Maven successfully collect the hot cross buns and **bounce back** through the window to safety?

Your job is to help Maven collect the hot cross buns and escape safely by placing bouncy pillows for him to use!
Each level features a different amount of pillows to use. Maven must collect all three hot cross buns without touching the floor in order to escape safely!
If Maven falls to the floor, he will get caught by the cross baker!!
If Maven doesn't get all 3 hot cross buns, he must try again until his heist is successful!
